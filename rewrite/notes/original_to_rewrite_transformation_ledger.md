## Removed or demoted material

Trading overlays, equity allocation, the emerging-market-native regime, and calibrated breadth propositions remain outside the reader builds. Strategy claims require executable forwards, costs, and frozen construction; the equity and EM-native applications dilute the central international-finance contribution; and the calibrated threshold relies on stronger assumptions than the empirical design. Recovered source crops remain external audit assets.

## Evidentiary boundary

The source-reported 10Y--2Y results cannot be independently replicated from the files in the workspace. By contrast, the public 10Y--3M and delivered-easing exercises are reproducible from public inputs and code, but they change the data, tenor, rule family, and/or timing. They therefore challenge and contextualize the baseline; they do not validate it as a replication.
