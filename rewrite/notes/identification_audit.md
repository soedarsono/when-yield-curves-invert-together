# Identification audit for the rewrite

## Claim that survives

In the inherited 1988--2026 analysis, a predetermined state built from fresh, synchronized non-dollar G10 yield-curve inversions predicts lower next-month carry returns, primarily through exchange-rate changes. The association is not confined to the worst return observations and is not spanned by the reported U.S.-curve, continuous-slope, NFCI, or VIX benchmarks.

## Interpretation that is supported but not identified

The currency-level loss is ordered by predetermined equity beta and is followed by weaker leading indicators. This is consistent with repricing of a common growth-sensitive currency characteristic. Beta can also proxy for commodity, liquidity, trade-network, intermediary, and dollar exposures. The return and macro evidence do not isolate one channel.

## Claims that should not appear without qualification

- “The signal identifies disaster probability.” It predicts a distributional state but does not separately identify probability, size, or price of disaster risk.
- “The signal causes currency crashes.” Yield curves and currencies respond jointly to common news.
- “The delayed forward is estimated.” The inherited model assumes lagged compensation; the Fama-slope exercise is not a clean estimate of that friction.
- “Loss vanishes at zero beta.” The fitted intercept is small, but uncertainty around the nine-currency cross-sectional fit is needed.
- “The downcurve is an inflation-normalization regime.” Shape, rate level, and inflation coincide in two historical blocks and are not separately identified.
- “The strategy is investable.” Money-market returns, in-sample rule evaluation, and stylized transaction costs do not establish feasible net returns.

## Decisive missing evidence

1. Original raw-data identifiers, cleaned series, event file, portfolio code, inference code, and output manifests.
2. Frozen-rule holdout or prospective evaluation.
3. Episode-level forest plot and full rule specification curve.
4. Country-level expected-rate/term-premium decompositions.
5. Feasible forward or futures implementation with bid-ask spreads, basis, financing, turnover, and instrument availability.
6. Independent data-vintage audit of the curve chronology.
