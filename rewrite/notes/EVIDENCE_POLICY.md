# Evidence policy for the reconstruction

1. A result from the source PDF is called **inherited**, never replicated or verified.
2. A public-data result must be generated by code, appear in the result ledger, and identify its data vintage, sample, unit, timing, and uncertainty.
3. A simulation must state its parameters and cannot be used as evidence that the empirical signal works.
4. Public shadow checks may test a mechanism, but they may not be described as validation of the unavailable proprietary panel.
5. If an independently generated check conflicts with the source paper, the manuscript reports the conflict; it does not search for a friendlier specification.
6. The main claim remains predictive and associational unless a design rules out the relevant alternatives.
7. The effective number of independent signal observations is the number of episodes, not the number of months.
8. Trading language is removed unless returns are based on feasible instruments and implementation costs.
9. Downcurve heterogeneity remains exploratory unless it travels beyond the two inflation-era clusters used to motivate it.
10. Every main-text number must resolve to a row in either the source-claim ledger or the generated result ledger.
