\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The cross-country configuration of yield curves predicts a distinct currency-return state. When fresh 10Y--2Y inversions are live in at least two non-U.S. G10 economies, next-month carry spot returns are 12.9 percentage points per year lower. The known interest differential absorbs only part of that depreciation. The opposite signs in the separate exactly-one and exactly-two indicator regressions, together with the portfolio horse race, distinguish the configuration from inversion in general, the U.S. curve, and the reported continuous average-slope and own-state measures. Those indicator coefficients are each measured against a different complement and are not a direct one-versus-two contrast.

The cross-section gives that forecast economic content. High-rate currencies lose relative to low-rate funding currencies, and bilateral losses grow with predetermined S\&P equity beta. Persistent portfolio cores show that the result is not created solely by rotation at signal dates. The S\&P-based exposure ordering is more stable across the reported tenor, release, and common-term-premium perturbations than the precise carry magnitude, although the nine-point fit is descriptive and the full-sample beta result depends on the equity benchmark.

An independently executable public 10Y--3M audit frequently produces a negative coefficient across its declared family, but no rule meets the 5-percent common-calendar rotation-reference criterion and the result attenuates after crisis-episode deletion. The public proxy therefore does not independently confirm the baseline relation. It shows why the exact carry coefficient cannot be treated as invariant to tenor, state persistence, or outcome construction. The main empirical object is the baseline configuration and the exposure-ordered loss it precedes.

The next decisive evidence is now well defined. Freeze the 10Y--2Y algorithm and vintage protocol, release the complete country-month state matrix, test each currency with its own curve excluded, and measure executable forward payoffs in the next unused episode. Those tests can determine whether the configuration is a durable international signal and whether its spot forecast translates into a negative conditional carry payoff.
